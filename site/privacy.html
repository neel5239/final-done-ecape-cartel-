Who we are

Escape Cartel is a travel company and trip planner run by Neel in India. This page explains what happens to the details you give us on this website and on WhatsApp.

Questions? WhatsApp or call +91 98257 44110.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms and trip builder. Everything except your name and WhatsApp number is optional.

WhatsApp

When you submit the form, the site does not post your details to a server. Instead it writes them into a WhatsApp message addressed to +91 98257 44110 and opens WhatsApp for you. Nothing reaches us until you press send.

Once you send a message, WhatsApp handles it under its own terms and privacy policy, and we can see your number and what you wrote. Chats stay on our business phone or account and are used only to plan your trip.

Browser storage and cookies

  • Your device only: your wishlist, compared trips, and a copy of the last enquiry you submitted are saved in your own browser (local storage). We cannot see them, and clearing your browser data removes them.
  • No third-party trackers by design: the site loads its own fonts and scripts from its own server. We do not set advertising cookies.
  • Visit statistics: our own server counts page views and taps on WhatsApp, call and join buttons, so we know which trips people look at. It sets no cookie and stores no name, phone or full IP address: a visitor is counted with a code that changes every day. Nothing is shared with other companies, and browsers set to "Do Not Track" are not counted. The counts are deleted after about 13 months.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and plan your trip.
  • To confirm bookings, dates and payments with you.
  • To contact you about the trip you asked about, and about changes to it.

Ask us on WhatsApp about any trip updates you want to receive.

Who sees it

We do not sell your details. If a trip requires hotels, transport providers or guides to receive information, we will explain what is shared before you book.

We may also share details if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Ask us on WhatsApp how long your enquiry or booking details are kept, or request deletion of your enquiry details.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it. Message us on WhatsApp or call, and we will act on it. You can also stop us contacting you at any time by telling us.

For questions about how we handle your information, contact us on WhatsApp or by phone.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, the date at the top will change. Related page: Terms and cancellations.

For the latest details about your information, contact us directly.
