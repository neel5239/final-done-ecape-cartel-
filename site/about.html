Our story

Built for people who want the trip, not the template.

Most trips ask you to fit the plan. Escape Cartel starts from the opposite end: your dates, your budget and the places you actually want to see.

Neel runs Escape Cartel as a travel company and trip planner in India. We plan small-group journeys for travellers who like company, and fully custom trips for those who want their own route.

Most of our travellers are students and young professionals travelling on a real budget, so we keep pricing plain and plans flexible. Browse a trip with a clear per-person budget or ask us to plan a private trip for your friends.

What we stand for

Four commitments behind every trip.

Promises about how we work, not claims about awards or numbers.

  • 01

    Your trip, your call

    Dates, stops, pace and budget are yours to shape. We suggest; you decide.

  • 02

    Straight talk on price

    A clear breakdown before you pay, with what is included and what is not.

  • 03

    Small groups, real people

    Groups stay small enough that everyone gets heard and no one gets lost.

  • 04

    Care on the road

    Someone reachable on WhatsApp before, during and after your trip.
