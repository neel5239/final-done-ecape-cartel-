About these terms

Escape Cartel is a travel company run by Neel in India. Ask us for the trip-specific terms in writing before you make a payment.

If any term is unclear, contact us before booking.

Prices and quotes

  • Prices are on request. The price that applies is the written quote we send you.
  • Ask for a breakdown of inclusions, exclusions and any applicable taxes before you pay.
  • The validity period of a quote is stated in that quote.

Payments

Review the full per-person budget and trip details before payment. For group trips, you confirm that you can pay the full budget yourself.

Ask us about accepted payment methods and any trip-specific terms before you pay.

Changing your dates

Tell us if your dates change. We will share available alternatives and any cost in writing.

  • Change window: ask us before booking.
  • Any change fee: confirmed in your written terms.
  • Any difference in trip price: confirmed before you accept a new date.

Cancellation and refunds

Cancellation and refund terms depend on the trip. Request the written schedule before you book, and contact us promptly if your plans change.

Custom and private trips

Custom trips may have different supplier and cancellation terms. Ask for the terms in your written quote.

Group departures

  • Ask us for the minimum group size and confirmed departure date.
  • If a departure changes, we will explain the available options directly.
  • The itinerary can change for weather, road closures or safety. We will tell you as soon as we know.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us correct names and contact details, and carry valid photo ID.
  • Tell us about any health condition, allergy or need that could affect the trip.
  • Follow the trip captain's safety instructions and respect local people, places and rules.
  • Behaviour that endangers others or the group may affect participation; ask for the trip-specific terms before booking.

Safety and risk

Travel, especially trekking and adventure activities, carries risks from weather, terrain and transport. Ask about safety arrangements and suitable insurance for your trip.
